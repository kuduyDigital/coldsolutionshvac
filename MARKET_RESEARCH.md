# Market Research — ColdSolutions HVAC/R (Houston & Corpus Christi, TX)

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · Sources at bottom. Items marked *(H)* are hypotheses, not evidence.

## Summary
Gulf Coast Texas is a cooling-dominant, high-humidity market where AC failure is urgent, and commercial refrigeration failure means lost inventory. Homeowners pick contractors mainly on **reviews/reputation and warranty**, with transparent pricing close behind. Local competitors look alike: blue palettes, "since 19XX", badge walls, coupons, and the same "Schedule / Call" header. ColdSolutions has two things they mostly lack: a **real three-generation refrigeration lineage** and **commercial refrigeration** next to residential HVAC, backed by unusually long warranties.

## Audience
1. **Homeowners (Houston metro, Corpus Christi)** — AC down in summer heat, aging system, or replacement shopping. Mobile, often stressed.
2. **Small commercial operators** — restaurants, convenience stores, grocers, offices: walk-in/reach-in coolers, RTUs. They care about downtime, response time and recurring maintenance.
3. **Property managers / landlords** *(H)* — multi-unit maintenance, predictable pricing.

## Journey hypotheses
| Stage | Homeowner | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | No cooling, high bill, failing system *(H)* | Cooler warm, RTU fault, inspection *(H)* |
| Evaluation | Google reviews (52% use online reviews — Mantel 2025), price transparency | Refrigeration capability, response time, availability |
| Trust | Reputation (92% important), warranty (86%) — ACHR/BNP 2025 | References from similar businesses *(H)*, licensing |
| Objection | Cost, upsell fear, "will they show up" *(H)* | Disruption, after-hours premium *(H)* |
| Conversion | Book online / call; instant quote for replacement | Call / quote form |
| Follow-through | Membership upsell, maintenance reminder | Service agreement |

Pricing: 70% more likely to call a contractor transparent on pricing; 69% search for pricing online but only 15% find it clear (myCLEARopinion, via ACHR). → Show membership benefits, warranty terms and starting points plainly.

## Incentives (compliance note)
Federal 25C/25D credits ended 2025-12-31. Texas HEAR/HOMES (~$690M) not yet distributing as of mid-2026; expected late 2026–2027. Utility rebates exist (CenterPoint in Houston; AEP Texas territory for Corpus Christi — *verify*). → Replace the site's "IRA rebate up to $2,000/$5,000" block with "Ask us about current utility rebates" until the client confirms.

## Design implications
1. Differentiate through **substance and tone**, not louder blue: calm, technical, confident.
2. Make the founder lineage a signature section (1926 → today) — authentic, not decorative.
3. Two-door IA: **Homes** / **Businesses**, with a persistent dual CTA (Request quote · Book service) and a city-aware phone.
4. Proof block = warranties + membership comparison + real reviews widget + real job photos.
5. Show license number, brands serviced, service areas explicitly (client to supply license).
6. Mobile-first: thumb-reachable sticky action bar.
