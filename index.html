Our story

Cold has been the family trade since 1926.

It started with a broken freezer in an ice-cream shop. Three generations later, the same habit — fix it properly, stand behind the work — runs every ColdSolutions job.

  1. 1926

    Jose Santiago De Santiago Olguin is born.

    He and his wife raise twelve children and run an ice-cream shop, churning by hand. He buys the town's first refrigeration system — and when it breaks, he fixes it himself. Soon he's known as the ice-cream man who fixes refrigeration.

  2. The next generation

    His son Alfredo rides along on service calls from a very young age, then opens his own refrigeration business in a bigger town in Zacatecas, Mexico.

  3. 2013

    Alfredo and his son found ColdSolutions.

    Heating, venting, air conditioning and commercial refrigeration — built on lasting relationships, quality service and reasonable prices.

  4. Today

    Homes and businesses across Houston and Corpus Christi — still on the roof, not behind a desk.

“With the most reputational team, within 10 years we became the number one choice for heating, venting, air conditioning, and refrigeration solutions of Texas.”

— ColdSolutions
